repainting every card and table for a rule that does not apply to them is not a
fix. The field edge is the token fields are drawn with (border-input in input,
textarea, select, checkbox).

and fails on any surface below 3:1. It also pins the alpha across every
ring-ring/N utility, re-checks 4.5:1 on nine text pairs, and asserts the old
values still fail so the gate cannot go vacuous.
